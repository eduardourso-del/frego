# Exploração opens the app

Exploração is the first tab. Lojas, Prêmios, Histórico, and Perfil follow, so opening the app lands on the city she is browsing. Lojas stays the list of Clubes she already belongs to. This supersedes the second-tab position in ADR 0013. Exploração is still not the Clube: Visível and Favorito stay as that decision recorded them.

## Considered Options

- **Lojas first.** Clubes she already belongs to are the home. Rejected: discovery is the front door, and the two lists stay separate either way.
- **Explorar first.** She lands on Exploração. Chosen.
