# Bônus de pontos e cashback pode dobrar o ganho

Pontos and cashback on a Pesquisa can be a fixed credit or a double of that earn. Carimbos stay a fixed count. The double is copied onto the Convite when the earn is recorded, so the page she already has open keeps that amount. An earn that did not credit that wallet does not open a Convite.

We rejected treating the quantity field as a multiplier, and doubling carimbos.
