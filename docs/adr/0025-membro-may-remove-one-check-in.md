# A Membro may remove one unconsumed Check-in

A Membro may take one Check-in off a Cliente when Resgatar has not consumed it. Her count drops by one. They may do that again. The civil day stays used, so she cannot Check-in again until the next day in the Fuso. Check-ins already consumed, and a Voucher already created, stay. The scan stays her act: the Balcão does not confirm each one. Removal is how the Estabelecimento walks back a card scanned from home.

## Considered Options

- **Nobody removes a Check-in.** Rejected: a new QR stops later scans and leaves a streak she can still Resgatar.
- **Clear her whole unconsumed count.** Rejected: one at a time can fix a single day without wiping a real Cliente.
