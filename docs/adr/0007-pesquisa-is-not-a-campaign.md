# Pesquisa is not a Campanha

A Pesquisa is the Estabelecimento's questions, not a program that entitles a Prêmio. One Cliente may give one Resposta. The Convite is a snapshot of the latest qualifying earn, and the page she already has open submits that snapshot. The Bônus is one wallet credit (shared carimbos or one Cartela, pontos, or cashback), not a Voucher. Undo of the sale does not take the Resposta or the Bônus back.

We rejected making Pesquisa a Campanha type, paying the Bônus on every earn, and a public link that anyone could submit.
