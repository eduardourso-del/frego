# Entrada follows her Cidade

An Estabelecimento becomes listed in Exploração when an operator has made it Visível and its status is trial or active. That transition sends one Entrada to each Cliente whose Cidade would list one of its Unidades: the same city, or within 15 km of that city's center. Clientes who already have a Clube there are left out. Visível while the Cadastro is still pending waits for approval. Turning Visível off and on again, adding a later Unidade, and a Cidade she sets afterward do not send another. Several Estabelecimentos on the same day each send their own Entrada.

## Considered Options

- **Every Cliente, when the Cadastro is approved.** Rejected: approval does not list the place, and her Cidade is what makes it available to her.
- **The city she is browsing.** Rejected: Exploração remembers that choice apart from her Cidade.
- **A daily digest.** Rejected: each Estabelecimento announces itself.
