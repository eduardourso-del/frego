# A Check-in is her scan, not a visita

A Campanha de check-in entitles its Prêmio by the Cliente scanning one QR for that Campanha, at most once per civil day in the Fuso. Only one is active at an Estabelecimento. The first scan opens her Clube. Place is not checked, and an Audiência does not limit it. It is not limited to a Unidade: a scan of that QR counts on the Clube. The phone camera opens an https link; with no app, that link sends her to install. The Estabelecimento can replace that QR. The old link stops counting, and Check-ins already recorded stay. The existing `visits` type stays a carimbo earn at the till. Pausing freezes new Check-ins and leaves the count, including a set she can still Resgatar. Archiving ends Check-ins she has not Resgatado. Making that Campanha active again does not bring them back. Resgates already counted toward “nesta campanha” stay counted. A Voucher already created can still be Confirmado.

## Considered Options

- **A compra at the till counts.** Rejected: the card is the Cliente's act, and visita already means a till visit.
- **The Balcão confirms each scan.** Rejected: the scan itself is the Check-in.
- **Her location must match a Unidade.** Rejected: the card is the control, and a QR indoors often fails that check. Her location stays unread.
- **Limit to some Unidades.** Rejected: place is not checked, so the limit would not stop a scan.
- **Reuse the `visits` type.** Rejected: that type earns carimbos.
- **Limit by Audiência.** Rejected: the card would lie to the next table.
- **Scan only from inside the app.** Rejected: the phone camera is how someone new starts.
