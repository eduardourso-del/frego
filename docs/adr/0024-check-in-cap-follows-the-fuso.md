# A Check-in cap follows the Fuso

The Resgatar cap on a Campanha de check-in uses the Estabelecimento's Fuso for the day, the week (Monday–Sunday), the month, and the year. Her Check-in is already one per civil day in that Fuso. A carimbo Campanha and a Promoção keep America/Sao_Paulo. In São Paulo the clocks match. In Rio Branco a São Paulo day would still be yesterday after her local midnight.

## Considered Options

- **America/Sao_Paulo, like the other caps.** Rejected: she could Check-in on a new local day and still be inside yesterday's cap.
