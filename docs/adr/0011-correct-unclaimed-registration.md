# Staff can move an unclaimed registration onto the right phone

The phone is the Cliente's account, so the till cannot rewrite it once she uses the app or belongs to another Estabelecimento. A registration that exists only at this Estabelecimento, with no app, can be corrected: the display name changes in place, and a different phone either renames that registration or folds this loja's membership — carimbos, pontos, cashback, and vouchers — into the Cliente who already owns the number. The display name is the same field the app edits.

We rejected editing every phone in place (it would take over a live account) and undoing only the last lançamento (the wrong registration would keep the number, and the next visit would still land there). A welcome may go to the corrected number; the message already sent to the wrong number cannot be recalled.
