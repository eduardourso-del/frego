# The Bônus wallet gates the Convite

A Pesquisa with a Bônus opens a Convite only when that earn credited the same wallet. Cashback requires cashback on the purchase, pontos require pontos, and carimbos require carimbos. A later earn on another wallet leaves an already-open Convite as it is. With no Bônus, any qualifying earn opens a Convite, and she can answer.

We rejected letting her answer and then skipping the credit. The Bônus is the reason the Convite exists, and a purchase on another wallet cannot pay it.
