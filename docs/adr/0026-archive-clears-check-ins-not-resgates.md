# Archive clears Check-ins, not Resgates

Archiving a Campanha de check-in ends Check-ins she has not Resgatado, and setting it active again starts her at zero. Resgates already counted toward “nesta campanha” stay counted. A fresh cap is a new Campanha. Day, week, month, and year caps keep following the Fuso.

## Considered Options

- **The cap starts over with the fresh Check-ins.** Rejected: bringing the Campanha back would mint another one-time Prêmio.
