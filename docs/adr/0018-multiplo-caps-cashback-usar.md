# The Múltiplo caps the cashback Usado

A Múltiplo on the Estabelecimento is a whole number from 2 up. On a compra, Usar can take at most the compra divided by that number, rounded down to the centavo, and never more than the saldo. A smaller amount is still allowed. With none set, Usar can take up to the compra. New cashback stays a percent of what she pays.

We rejected freezing the whole saldo until the compra covers N times all of it, and requiring what she pays to be N times the cashback. The compra itself is the multiple: R$ 10 Usado needs a compra of at least R$ 30 when the Múltiplo is 3, and a compra of R$ 24 can still Usar R$ 8.
