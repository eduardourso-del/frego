# A stamp Campanha may keep a Cartela

Shared carimbos let a cheap earn Resgatar an expensive Prêmio. A stamp Campanha can instead keep a Cartela: carimbos earned on it Resgatar only that Prêmio. The choice is made before the Campanha is first activated, then it stays. Shops that want one pile leave the Campanha without a Cartela.

We rejected pricing both prizes on the shared pile (a coffee earn could still buy the lunch) and named carimbo types (two prizes cannot share a card unless they both stay on the shared pile).
