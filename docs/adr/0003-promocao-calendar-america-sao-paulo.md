# Promoção calendar and quota use America/Sao_Paulo

Day, week, month, year, shop dates, and weekdays for a Promoção are calendar bounds in `America/Sao_Paulo`, matching intelligence, not UTC.

Birthday’s “once per year” is UTC. Midnight UTC is 21:00 in São Paulo, so a UTC “per day” cap would flip during evening service. We accepted two clocks rather than move birthday or invent per-business timezones.
