# A used-up lifetime cap stops new Check-ins

A scan is a Check-in only when that Campanha can still entitle a later Resgate. Once “nesta campanha” is used up, the next scan records nothing. Check-ins she already holds stay until she Resgata what the cap allows, a Membro removes one, or the Campanha is archived. A day, week, month, or year cap still allows today’s Check-in, and the set waits for the next period. She may hold more Check-ins than she can still collect. Raising the cap so another Resgate is possible lets scans count again.

## Considered Options

- **Every scan still counts.** Rejected: the number would climb on a Campanha she can no longer collect.
